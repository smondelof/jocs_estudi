Verb Hunt

Can you find the six verbs?

Read a short story. It has 3 regular verbs and 3 irregular verbs in the past. Tap the verbs to find them. Be careful: tapping a word that is not a verb is a mistake. Then say if each verb is regular or irregular and write its present form. Every game has a new story.

Step 1 of 2 · Find the verbs
Tap a word to mark it as a verb.
Step 2 of 2 · Regular or irregular?

For each verb, choose regular or irregular and write the present (infinitive).
